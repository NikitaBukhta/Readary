# Credits

## Twemoji

Color emoji glyphs rendered in the app come from the
[Twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) graphics set, vendored under
[assets/emoji/](assets/emoji/) and bundled into the app at compile time via
the `:/emoji/<codepoint>.svg` qrc resource.

Twemoji graphics are licensed under
[CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) — the full text
ships with the assets as [assets/emoji/LICENSE-GRAPHICS.txt](assets/emoji/LICENSE-GRAPHICS.txt).
